## **1. The Doctrine of Divine Order and Spiritual Law**

> *God operates by divine patterns and spiritual laws — grace works through these principles, not apart from them.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **Genesis 8:22** – The enduring law of “seedtime and harvest” signifies that divine results are produced by set principles.
* **Luke 1:1–4** – Luke “sets in order” the truths of the Gospel for Theophilus; revelation must follow divine order to yield authority.
* **Genesis 1:28** – Dominion, fruitfulness, and multiplication are bound to God’s ordained processes.
* **2 Chronicles 16:9** – God shows Himself strong to those whose hearts are aligned with His order.

**Explanation:**
Grace does not nullify divine principles; it operates through them. A believer aligned with the *order* of God’s Spirit functions in power and authority. Spiritual maturity therefore requires not just zeal, but understanding of the laws that connect *seed to harvest*, *faith to fruit*, and *grace to manifestation*.

## **2. The Doctrine of the Heart: The Wellspring of Grace**

> *Grace transforms and empowers the heart; everything God does in man flows from within.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **Proverbs 4:23** – “Keep your heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.”
* **Ezekiel 36:26** – God gives a new heart and a new spirit.
* **2 Chronicles 16:9** – God searches for hearts made perfect toward Him.
* **Hebrews 13:9** – “It is a good thing that the heart be established with grace.”

**Explanation:**
In salvation, God begins with the heart — transforming its nature and establishing it in grace. All divine fruit—faith, love, power, vision—springs from this internal renewal. When the heart is grounded in grace, it becomes the channel of divine life, the seat of faith, and the foundation of spiritual authority.

## **3. The Doctrine of Grace as the Revelation of Christ**

> *Grace is not a concept; it is the person and ministry of Jesus Christ revealed.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **John 1:17** – “The law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.”
* **1 Corinthians 10:4** – “That Rock was Christ.”
* **Colossians 1:5–6** – The message of grace “brings forth fruit and grows by its own inherent power.”
* **Titus 2:11–12** – The grace of God teaches men to deny ungodliness.
* **Philippians 2:13** – “It is God who works in you both to will and to do of His good pleasure.”

**Explanation:**
Grace is not permission to sin but the divine power that teaches holiness and produces fruit. It is Christ Himself at work in the believer. The *message of grace* bears fruit inherently, without manipulation or force, because the Word carries its own power. True ministry, therefore, flows from *Christ revealed*, not human effort.

## **4. The Doctrine of Repentance through Divine Goodness**

> *Grace reveals that repentance is a response to love, not to fear or wrath.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **Romans 2:4** – “The goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance.”
* **James 1:20** – “The wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.”
* **Psalms 130:4** – “There is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared.”
* **Acts 13:38** – “Through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins.”
* **Romans 3:8** – Paul’s critics claim he preaches “let us do evil that good may come”—proof that grace was misunderstood even then.

**Explanation:**
The sermon underscores that fear and condemnation cannot produce true repentance. Transformation flows from *revelation of God’s goodness*, *forbearance*, and *forgiveness*. This shifts the focus from human striving to divine love. When grace is taught correctly, it leads men to holiness born of gratitude and awe—not guilt or fear.

## **5. The Doctrine of Love: The Core of Grace**

> *Grace reveals the heart of God as unconditional love, which never fails.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **John 3:16** – God’s ultimate expression of love and grace through giving.
* **1 Corinthians 13:8** – “Love never fails.”

**Explanation:**
At the center of grace is divine love (*agape*). Love empowers patience, forgiveness, and restoration; it is the inexhaustible energy of the kingdom. Because God *is* love, grace can never fail. Any gospel lacking love’s expression is incomplete—it becomes law, not grace.

## **6. The Doctrine of Faith and Righteousness**

> *Grace justifies the believer apart from works, imputing righteousness through faith.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **Romans 3:23–24** – “All have sinned… being justified freely by His grace.”
* **Romans 4:2–3** – Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.
* **Psalms 39:5** – “Every man at his best state is altogether vanity.”
* **Luke 18:10–14** – Parable of the Pharisee and the Publican—justification by humility, not performance.
* **Romans 5:8** – “While we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”

**Explanation:**
Human effort can never justify. Even the most devout works are “filthy rags” without grace. Faith alone receives righteousness, because grace is what enables faith to operate. This dismantles spiritual pride and exalts Christ as the sole basis of justification.

## **7. The Doctrine of Spiritual Power through Grace**

> *True power and ministry flow from yieldedness to God’s grace, not self-effort.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **Ephesians 1:19–20** – God’s power that raised Christ is at work in believers.
* **1 Corinthians 15:10** – “I labored more abundantly… yet not I, but the grace of God.”
* **Galatians 3:1–5** – Miracles occur not by works, but by “the hearing of faith.”
* **Philippians 2:12–13** – God works within us to will and to do.
* **Revelation 2:14** – The “doctrine of Balaam” warns of spiritual corruption through wrong teaching.

**Explanation:**
Power, miracles, and endurance in ministry are the works of grace, not human might. Grace builds the church, multiplies believers, and sustains the anointing. Legalism is spiritual witchcraft—attempting to perfect by works what began by grace. True power flows only from rest and reliance on divine ability.

---

## **8. The Doctrine of Transformation by Grace: From Law to Liberty**

> *Grace is the divine transition from man’s striving under law to resting in Christ’s finished work.*

**Key Scriptures:**

* **John 1:17** – The law was given by Moses; grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.
* **1 Corinthians 10:4** – Christ, the Rock that followed them, symbolizes ongoing grace.
* **Romans 4:2–3** – Faith replaces legal striving.
* **Galatians 3:1–5** – Returning to works after grace is “bewitchment.”

**Explanation:**
Law exposes sin; grace transforms the sinner. The grace of Christ is continual, pursuing, and self-sustaining (“the Rock followed them”). Under grace, believers live by faith in Christ’s finished work, not by observing rituals. Grace empowers inward transformation rather than external compliance.
